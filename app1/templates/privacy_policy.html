Feadl.io Privacy Policy

Welcome to Feadl.io! Your autonomous social media manager. We value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy clarifies how we handle, store, and protect your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We gather data to offer a tailored and efficient service:

  • Account credentials, such as username and password.
  • Social media credentials needed for post creation and scheduling.
  • Contact information, predominantly your email address.
  • Content you introduce into the app, including messages, photos, and videos.
  • Usage data like login timestamps and feature usage patterns.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

We utilize your data to:

  • Facilitate the core functions of the app.
  • Enhance user experience and optimize app performance.
  • Send updates, alerts, and promotional content.
  • Conduct market research and analytics.

3. Data Security

We're dedicated to ensuring the security of your data:

  • Data is stored on secure, encrypted servers.
  • We use SSL/TLS for data transfer.
  • Regular audits to monitor for vulnerabilities.

4. Third-Party Involvement

We might incorporate third-party tools for better service:

  • These tools follow their privacy practices.
  • We don't share your data without explicit consent.
  • Review third-party privacy policies for clarity.

5. User Rights

You're in control of your data:

  • Access or request a copy of your data anytime.
  • Modify inaccuracies or incomplete data.
  • Request data deletion under certain circumstances.

6. Policy Modifications

Occasionally, we may revise this policy. Regularly review for updates.

7. Reach Out

Queries or concerns? Email us at team@feadl.io
